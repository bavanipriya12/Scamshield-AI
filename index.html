      BEFORE YOU REPLY

      Give yourself a moment.

      Scam messages often try to rush you. A pause gives you time to check what is really going on.

      • 01 Don’t tap links or open unexpected files.
      • 02 Never share passwords, PINs, or one-time codes.
      • 03 Check with the person or organization another way.

      SMALL HABITS, STRONGER SAFETY

      A few things worth remembering

      01

      Keep your codes to yourself

      A real support agent should never ask you to tell them your password, PIN, or one-time passcode.

      02

      Find your own way there

      Instead of using a link in a surprise message, open the official app or type the website address yourself.

      03

      Verify the request separately

      Call a known number or use a contact method you already trust—not details from the message.

      i

      Think you’ve lost money to online fraud in India? Contact your bank promptly and call the national cybercrime helpline at 1930. You can also report it at cybercrime.gov.in.
